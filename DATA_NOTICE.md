## `mp_20`

`data/mp20/mp_20.csv` is the frozen input used by the reported local experiments. Its SHA-256 is `c0f55956e7d0615c15d0c2044865465cdb02846d69e0b3091d7b9c8ffb65c63a`. The file is derived from Materials Project data. Users should cite the Materials Project database and follow its applicable access and reuse terms.

The three files under `data/mp20/benchmarks/` are research snapshots generated for the controlled-contamination and robustness experiments. They are provided to make the reported analyses inspectable. The repository does not claim ownership of the underlying upstream records.

## Alloy workbooks

The 185-record and 315-record workbooks under `data/alloys/` are structured tables compiled from public literature. The manuscript cites:

1. K. Xu et al., "Intelligent design of Fe-Cr-Ni-Al/Ti multi-principal element alloys based on machine learning", *Journal of Materials Research and Technology*, 35:6864-6873, 2025.
2. B. Wang et al., "Rapid in situ alloying of CoCrFeMnNi high-entropy alloy from elemental feedstock toward high-throughput synthesis via laser powder bed fusion", *Frontiers of Mechanical Engineering*, 18(1):11, 2023.
3. X. Chen et al., "Introduction of nanoprecipitation into supersaturated CoCrFeNiMo0.5 alloy prepared by laser-powder bed fusion via microstructure tailoring to achieve excellent properties", *Scripta Materialia*, 235:115636, 2023.

The workbooks do not contain complete row-level source identifiers for every record. Their presence in this reproduction repository does not assert a new license over the source publications. Confirm institutional and publisher requirements before redistributing the workbooks outside the paper-review context.
